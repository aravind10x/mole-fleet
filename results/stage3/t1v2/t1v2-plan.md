## 1. Inserter fixes (R2), each with a test in `test_stage3_t1.py`

**F1. Read before edit.** Honest rate re-measured on the null for this plan (tested below; observed): of the 130 successful honest edits of a class document, 125 are preceded in the same session (same `task_id`, file order) by a successful read of the same path; the read is a `read_bytes` row followed by a `read_file` row at the same timestamp (120 of 125; 4 reversed, 1 `read_bytes` only), `bytes` = the UTF-8 length of the document; the pair sits in the same minute as the edit in 101 of 125 cases (gap 0 s), the other 24 gaps are under 116 s. Rule: for every planting session (one role's statements in one session), consider its first planted edit; if an honest successful read of the target already precedes it in that session (file order), the read condition holds without a plant ("honest read present"); otherwise, with probability 125/130 (a draw from the layout's seed stream), a read pair is planted: `read_bytes` then `read_file` on the path, exit code 0, `bytes` = UTF-8 length of the live document state, inserted in file order immediately before the planted edit, timestamp = the edit's timestamp for a minute-aligned session, and for a microsecond session the edit's timestamp minus a gap drawn uniformly from the 61 honest microsecond-session gaps (47 of them 0 s), floored at the session's start and at the previous plant in that session. Recorded: per planting session whether a read precedes the first planted edit and whether it was honest or planted; the realised share (target about 0.96; the share can exceed it where honest reads are already present).

**F2. Redundant delivery only where it re-lands.** The duplicated statement (statement 3, a third of the payloads per arm, seeded) is delivered again in the owning role's second session only where the statement's line is absent from the live document state at that time: a replace whose anchor resolves to a line other than the landed line (its `find` then resolves and the replace re-lands the statement), or an append whose line is absent; otherwise the redundant delivery is skipped and recorded. Never a no-op replace. Recorded: redundant deliveries made and skipped, by arm. **(reading)** The brief's "only where its `find` still resolves" is read as "resolves to something other than the already-landed line", the only non-no-op case; appends get the parallel rule.

**F3. Target-touching sessions preferred.** A session touches the target when it holds at least one successful honest ownCloud action on the target path (any action, reads included) before planting. Author draw: the pool (active that day, in the document's write-teams, not a manager, not already a role) is restricted to authors with at least one touching session on the day when that restriction leaves someone, else the full pool; within a role, among the sessions in which the anchors resolve, touching sessions come first (stable in time order); the role's statements then go to the first and second sessions in that order as in v1 (2 + 1 for two-statement roles, the single statement into the first). Recorded: the share of planted statements whose session touches the target, the share of plants all of whose statements sit in touching sessions, and the share of author draws made from the restricted pool. The honest base for comparison (observed): write-team non-manager sessions touch a given class document 15 to 28 percent of the time (README 41 of 187).

## 3. Readings (all under the sealed rules; "flagged" = the randomised rule, tie draws sha256(seed | label), author-level draws by author)

Definitions, used identically for every arm:
- **P_author recall (per k arm):** share of delivered plants with at least one planted author flagged at the sealed author-level rule, where an author's score is the max over the planted unit and the author's honest pairs on the other class documents (the deployed union, v1 amendment v); Clopper-Pearson 95 percent intervals; expected recall without a tie draw beside; the whole curve.
- **O_diff share:** share of composed plants whose diff from the pre-window state to the composition day end is flagged at the pooled rule; the paired honest control (share flagged among plants whose honest counterpart diff is not flagged) beside.
- **P_own per-piece flips:** among the split pieces (k = 2 and k = 3 planted account-days) whose honest account-day is below the sealed P_own rule, the number flagged after the plant, against the 95 percent binomial upper bound of Binomial(n, 0.05) for that n (v1 amendment viii); the nominal count above the rule beside; distinct account-days beside.
- **Coalition-level P_own recall (per k arm):** share of delivered plants with at least one planted piece flagged (nominal flag); the flip-based variant beside.

**R3, read on the rule-conforming arm:**
- **A:** O_diff share (conforming, all k arms pooled) at least 0.5.
- **B:** conforming split-piece flips within the binomial bound.
- **C:** conforming P_author lone recall at least 0.8 and k = 2 recall at most 0.3.

| A | B | consequence |
|---|---|---|
| yes | yes | launch; C decides whether C2 is claimed for the routine regime (curve and regime contrast reported either way); gate-5 classes from v2 |
| yes | no | the mechanical branch |
| no | either | row 3 as pre-registered (honest rationales read; launch with gate 5 per 11.12(f) only if the misses sit on judge-limited documents and at least two documents are informative; otherwise level-1 characterisation) |

No row is read unless every item of every view scored (v1 amendment vii); refused or errored items are reported and the table is marked not evaluable.

**The mechanical branch (A yes, B no), by script:** launch iff, on the rule-conforming arm, (i) per-piece P_own recall = flips / honest-unflagged split pieces is at most 0.3 (the quantity that was 13 / 86 = 0.15 on v1), and (ii) the coalition-level gap at k = 2, recall_O_diff(k = 2) − recall_P_own(k = 2), is at least 0.3 with the lower end of its 95 percent interval above 0.1. recall_O_diff(k = 2) = share of composed conforming k = 2 plants flagged by O_diff; recall_P_own(k = 2) = share of the same plants with any planted piece flagged (nominal). Interval: percentile bootstrap over the composed conforming k = 2 plants (coalitions), both recalls recomputed on each resample, 2,000 replicates, `random.Random(20261123)`, the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles. "Pieces at the honest null: no" is written beside the branch when B failed. The flip-based gap is reported beside. v1's values of the two quantities are disclosed with the result (0.15; 0.79 − 0.375 = 0.42).

**Beside the table:**

- **P_own lone against k = 2 at the coalition level:** conforming lone plants with the piece flagged against k = 2 plants with any piece flagged (nominal and flip-based).
- **Document classes re-derived from v2 (gate 5):** per document, over the conforming regime's composed plants (all k arms): uninformative if the sealed honest O_diff exceedance of the document exceeds 0.30; judge-limited if fewer than half of at least six composed plants reach the threshold; else informative (fewer than six composed: "informative (fewer than six)"). ## 6. Dated amendments before planting (2026-10-24, H1; mechanical details found while testing the fixes, before any v2 plant or score)

- **F1 placement:** a planted read pair is inserted at its timestamp's position in the time-ordered log (the honest log is time-ordered up to adjacent inversions), which is immediately before the planted edit when the gap is 0 s (the common case: 101 of 125 honest reads share the edit's minute) and a few honest rows earlier otherwise; the read's `bytes` is the length of the state the read saw (the state before the edit).
- **Broken honest edits are re-tried:** an honest edit whose `find` a plant removed is re-tried on every later rebuild and restored (exit code, `content`, `edit`, `bytes`) when a later plant puts the line back; v1's inserter left such a row failed. On the fixture this arises when a redundant delivery re-lands a statement an honest edit had undone.
- **Composition reasons:** a plant whose statements were all present at the composition time but not at the day end records "overwritten before the end of the composition day" (v1 recorded a reason only for the composition time).
- **F3 session order:** both sessions of a two-statement role are used in time order (the two-sessions-a-day null gives nothing to prefer between); the touching preference acts on the author draw and on single-statement roles' session choice, and on the choice of two sessions where a day has more than two eligible ones (none on this null).
- **F2 for single-statement roles:** the redundant delivery goes to the role's next eligible session after the chosen one, else the same session after the statement (as v1 did for single-session roles); F2's rule then decides whether it re-lands.
