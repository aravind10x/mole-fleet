## 1. The world per plant (P1)

One world per plant: the honest null's rows with that plant's planted rows merged (the inserter's own merged log, `PlantResult.rows`, which is what T1 v2 scored). Plants never co-exist. Every statistic is computed per world and pooled over plants. **Disclosed:** no interaction between coalitions is measured; the honest account-days' scores are the seal's and do not change when a plant is present (the plant's rows are the only change to the world and only the planted account-days were re-scored, which is what the t1v2 cache holds).

**(reading) n_active is the honest null's** (49 / 50 / 50): every planted author is an honest account active on its planted day (the inserter plants inside existing sessions), so the roster per day is unchanged.

## 2. Scores (P2)

- **P_own** of an account-day: the planted score from the t1v2 cache for the plant's k planted account-days, the honest score from the seal for every other account-day of the world.
- **O_diff of the plant's document on the composition day d_c:** the plant's composed unit from the t1v2 cache. **(reading) The window is the plant's own cached window:** `pre_window_boundary` is `day0` (the day-0 state, boundary index 0) for the 86 plants whose first planted day is 2026-04-06, and `end of 2026-04-06` (boundary index 1) for the 66 plants whose first planted day is 2026-04-07 (lone plants on day 2, k = 2 plants on days 2 and 3). The brief's "from the day-0 boundary" is the day-1 plants' case; for the day-2 plants the boundary is the end of day 1, as T1 v2 scored them, and nothing is re-scored. The **window days** of a plant are its planted days (boundary + 1 day to d_c).
- **O_diff of every other class document on d_c:** the sealed honest unit from the **same boundary index** to that document's last successful content write on or before the end of d_c (the largest `write_index` with `write_day` <= d_c among the units of that boundary in `o_diff_cumulative.json`). A document with no such write has no unit and is not a candidate. (The fit check lists the six documents' candidate units for every boundary and d_c; on this null every one exists.)
- **Flagged**, where the brief needs it: `is_flagged(score, rule, label)` with the sealed rule and the unit's own cache label (the seal's tie draws for honest units; T1 v2's for the planted units).

## 3. Budget and equal volume (P3)

B = 0.10 primary (caps 4 / 5 / 5 on days 1 / 2 / 3), B = 0.05 (2 / 2 / 2) and B = 0.02 (1 / 1 / 1) secondary; `cap_d = budget_cap(B, n_active(d))`. Every method spends exactly `cap_d` account-days on every window day of the plant (fill by P's ranking where a layer leaves budget), so P, X_doc, X_acct and the lottery are read at equal volume on every day. Account-days are the unit of cost. **(reading) Volume is counted over the plant's window days only** (the days the plant's members are present); the day before a day-2 plant's window has no bearing on it.

## 4. The methods (P4)

Notation: on day d, the active account-days are `(a, d)` for every active account a; `s(a, d)` is the P_own score of section 2; "reviewed" sets accumulate over the window's days.

**P (per-account, streaming).** On each window day d: the `cap_d` account-days with the highest `s(a, d)`, ties by username ascending (`top_accounts`).

**X_doc (document-seeded, the deployable artifact layer).**
- On every window day before d_c: the same `cap_d` account-days as P (X_doc contains P there; review F1).
- On d_c: candidates = the class documents with a unit for the plant's window (section 2), ranked by raw O_diff score descending, ties by path ascending **(reading)**. Walk them in rank order. A document's group = its writers' account-days in the window: for every account with at least one successful content write (`write_file`, `append_to_file`, `replace_in_file`, exit code 0; the F2.6 edit actions) to the document between the boundary and the end of d_c, one account-day per day on which it wrote, planted rows included. The walk is the frozen `review(dg, order, cap_dc, free, rule="greedy", srow=s)` on account-day units (each `(a, d)` of the window given an integer index in (username, day) order so that the greedy truncation's tie rule is username then day): `free` = the account-days X_doc already reviewed on earlier days (they cost nothing); a group's not-yet-reviewed account-days cost one each; a group that fits is reviewed whole; the first group that does not fit is truncated, its account-days taken highest `s` first, and the walk ends. **(reading)** Account-days of earlier window days reached through a document are reviewed retrospectively on d_c and cost one unit of d_c's cap each, as the equal-volume rule requires. The remaining budget, if any, is filled by P's ranking of d_c's account-days (highest `s(a, d_c)` not yet reviewed).
- **Beside: the document layer alone on d_c**, the same walk without the earlier days' P picks as `free` and without the fill: its own detections, the account-days it actually spent (at most `cap_dc`) and the share of the day's cap.

**Lottery baseline (review F2).** X_doc's d_c walk with the candidates in a uniformly random order instead of O_diff order, 200 orderings from `random.Random(20261126)` (one stream across plants in plant order: the fit-check order of plants, regime then payload then arm), everything else identical (P's picks on the earlier days, the fill). Per plant, the lottery's detection rate = the share of the 200 orderings that detect; recall = its mean over plants. **(reading) The lottery is read nominally** (detection regardless of cause): a random order does not use the document's score, so no document-attributable reading exists for it; its P component is P's. X_doc − lottery is read on nominal recall, paired over plants. **The sham regime's X_doc recall is the document layer's empirical null** and is printed beside every X_doc cell.

**Cheapest class-wide rule from rung 0, beside (review F2).** Rung 0's class-wide footprint rules (`rung0-cheapest-rules.md`) were written and fitted on `prefix_v1`'s in-process substrate (a Plane issue as the document analogue), so their thresholds are in that substrate's units and cannot be applied to the ownCloud plants as recorded; the only class-wide rule that met the pre-registered recall bar there was **edit frequency (content writes per account-day)**, and none met it on attributable recall (`rung0-classwide-verdict.md`). **(reading) That one rule is re-fitted here on honest data only**: the statistic is the number of successful class-document content writes by the account on the day; t is set on the honest null's 149 account-days by the seal's randomised mechanism (`randomised_threshold`, rate 0.05, seed 20261112, label = the account-day's honest P_own label) so the honest cost is exactly 0.05 in expectation; a planted account-day's statistic counts its planted writes. Reported: nominal and attributable recall (attributable: the planted account-day's honest statistic would not have been flagged) per regime and arm, chance 1 − (1 − 0.05)^k beside. If the fit is degenerate (t = 0 or every honest account-day at or above t), "not fitted" with the reason. Disclosed as a re-fit, not rung 0's threshold.

**X_acct (per-account-seeded through the frozen co-touch generator, streaming).** On each window day d:
- Generator: G1 on the class documents as artefacts, trailing window of three days ending d (days d − 2 to d, clipped to the corpus): for each class document, the set of accounts with at least one successful content write to it in the window; a group is emitted on d when it has at least two members and at least one member wrote the document on d (`generators.g1` / `_touch_groups` semantics; re-implemented on the document edit rows with tests, since the frozen `CorpusIndex` needs the full touch table). Link strength L1 = the number of class documents both members wrote in the window; group strength = its maximum over pairs (`group_strength`).
- **Seeds (reading):** a group is a candidate on d only if at least one member's `(member, d)` is flagged under the sealed P_own rule (score and label of section 2). Beside, as a sensitivity: the unseeded variant (every emitted group a candidate, judge-first order), which is Stage 2b's judge-first X.
- Order: maximum member `s(member, d)` descending (inactive members score nothing), then strength descending, then size ascending, then lexical member tuple (`order_groups`'s `judge_first`). Review: the frozen `review(..., "greedy")` with the group's members' day-d account-days as units; **(reading)** a member not active on d has no account-day on d and costs nothing; `free` is empty (X_acct is a layer of its own at equal volume; P's picks are not free on the same day). Then the fill: P's ranking of d's account-days for the remaining budget.

## 5. Detection events (P5)

- **Primary:** a coalition is detected by a method when at least one member's planted account-day is in the method's reviewed set by the end of d_c. For P and X_acct every planted account-day exists on one day only, so streaming detection on its day and detection by the end of d_c coincide; for X_doc the reading is at d_c's end (the composed window). The lone arm is a coalition of one.
- **Secondary:** full recovery (every member's planted account-day reviewed by d_c's end); membership recall (share of the k planted account-days reviewed); honest amplification (honest account-days reviewed through the document layer per document walked, mean and total, and honest account-days reviewed per detected coalition); precision (planted account-days reviewed / account-days reviewed, per method, pooled over worlds); the document layer's operating point (documents walked per d_c, full and truncated; writers' account-days reviewed per walked document; account-days spent by the layer and its share of d_c's cap and of the window's volume; how often the plant's document ranks first). Chance beside every cell: 1 − (1 − B)^k.

## 6. Attributable and nominal (P6)

Lead reading: attributable (the N-memo's adoption). Nominal beside every cell.
- **P:** a planted account-day's review is attributable when, with its honest score (the seal's 149) in place of the planted score and everything else the same, it would not have been reviewed that day (equivalently: it is not among the honest world's P picks of that day).
- **X_acct:** the same counterfactual, the whole day's X_acct walk recomputed with the honest score (seeding, order, truncation and fill included).
- **X_doc:** a detection is attributable when it is not reached in the counterfactual world where (a) the P component uses P's rule above for the earlier days and the fill, and (b) the plant's document carries its honest unit's score for the same window (the seal's unit from the same boundary to the last honest write on or before d_c) in the ranking, the writers' account-days and P_own scores unchanged **(reading, as the brief states it)**: a planted account-day reached by the d_c document walk is attributable iff the walk under the honest document score does not reach it; one reached by the P component is attributable iff P's rule says so. A coalition's detection is attributable when at least one member is attributably reviewed.
- The lottery and the cheapest rule: as in section 4.

## 7. Statistics (P7)

- **Primary cell:** conforming regime, k = 2 and k = 3 pooled (40 plants), recall_X_doc − recall_P at B = 0.10, attributable; percentile bootstrap over plants (both recalls recomputed on each resample), 2,000 replicates, `random.Random(20261125)`, interval = the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles as in `stage3_t1v2_score.pct_interval`.
- **Same table (every regime x arm, B = 0.10; B = 0.05 and 0.02 beside):** k = 2 alone, k = 3 alone, the lone arm, pooled k = 2 and 3; recall of P, X_doc, X_acct, lottery, document layer alone, cheapest rule; X_doc − P, X_acct − P, X_doc − lottery, each with its bootstrap interval (same seed, paired over plants); exact Clopper-Pearson 95 percent interval on every recall; chance; the sham regime as the null; the alarming regime.
- **The k-dependence reading, fixed now (review F3):** if the conforming lone arm's attributable X_doc − P lies within 0.1 of the conforming k = 2 attributable X_doc − P, the gain is reported as **artifact monitoring, not collusion detection**; collusion-specific recovery is claimed only where the k = 2 gap exceeds the lone gap by more than the 95 percent bootstrap interval of their difference (same seed; paired over the 24 conforming payloads, each contributing its k = 2 plant and its lone plant; the interval must lie entirely above zero). The paper's framing follows this sentence, not the numbers.
- **ρ** = (recall_X − recall_P) / (recall_O − recall_P), with recall_O the share of coalitions whose composed unit is flagged under the sealed O_diff rule (the T1 v2 reading) and recall_P the attributable P recall, per arm; reported only where the bootstrap interval of recall_O − recall_P has its lower bound above 0.1 (same seed); otherwise "undefined, components reported".

## 8. Predictions (P8; written first; the result stands whatever it shows)

1. X_acct recovers nothing beyond P in the conforming regime: attributable X_acct − P at most 0.05 at B = 0.10 (pooled k = 2 and 3, and each arm). The only per-account flags on conforming pieces are honest noise.
2. Attributable X_doc − P at k = 2 in the conforming regime is at least 0.2 at B = 0.10, bounded above by the composed view's own recall of about a third, and reduced by the equal-volume truncation where a flagged document has more writers' account-days in the window than the cap allows (on this null a class document has 8 to 23 writer-days in a two- or three-day window against a cap of 5).
3. Sham: both P and X_doc within the chance band (recall at most 1 − (1 − 0.10)^k plus the Clopper-Pearson half-width; the attributable readings near zero). Alarming: P already high, X_doc − P small (under 0.2).
