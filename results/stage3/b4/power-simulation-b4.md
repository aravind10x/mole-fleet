**The condition used (brief 19 B4, fixed before any count).** A script insertion is anchored at a boundary of the item pair's own write stream (the day's start, or just after one of the day's successful content writes to either document, in file order) when both of the item's lines are present in their documents' states at that boundary, by name (`stage3_b4r.item_line`, primary) or by name or one of the row's Z5 entities (`stage3_b4r.row_entities`, sensitivity), and the pair is a read-together group with maintenance support (constraint 5). ## D. The simulation

**Model.** For each design (n test changes per cell, 1 or 2 placements on different accounts and days) and prior set, 300 experiments, seed 20261190. Every arm draws flags at its view's recall at the sealed 0.10 threshold. A test change's placements share a propensity drawn Beta(recall, ICC). Under item reuse, the test changes on one item share an item-level propensity, Beta(recall, item ICC), drawn independently per cell; that is conservative for contrasts paired by item. Intervals are percentile bootstraps with 1,000 replicates: payload-clustered (test changes with their placements), or two-stage (items with replacement, then test changes within each drawn item, item draws shared across the arms of one experiment) when the item is carried as a cluster. One same-shape control arm of n test changes is read by each view. Headroom is read on the higher cell against the control under the carrying view (O_diff on (1,1) against the control's per-document unit; the isolated dossier on (1,2); O_ws on (2,2)); below it the reading is *floor*. Above it, the round-6 partition applies on the 0.3 margin. C-ws pairs the two views on the same (2,2) test changes. The other contrasts compare different test changes.

**Truths.** C-frag: (1,2) = (1,1) − Δ for Δ = 0.3 and 0.45, and the prior-implied (1,2) = 1 − (1 − e)², two document units each caught at the one-end rate. C-split: (2,2) = (1,2) − Δ for Δ = 0, 0.3 and 0.45, and the implied two-account value. C-ws: the dossier on (2,2) = O_ws − 0.3, at each of O_ws's three values, and the implied value. A truth the prior cannot reach (a negative recall, for example C-frag 0.45 under Sonnet's 0.34) is marked n/a.

**Decidable** means the reading lands outside *floor* and *inconclusive* (present, attenuated or excluded). The design rule: the smallest n × placements at which the minimum, over the primary contrasts' named truths (C-frag 0.3 and 0.45; C-split 0, 0.3 and 0.45; C-ws 0.3 with O_ws equal to O_diff), is at least 0.8. P(present | true 0.3) cannot exceed about one half, because the point estimate falls below the margin half the time when the truth sits on it; such readings are *attenuated*, which counts as decidable.

- **Under the central priors: not sized.** No design in the grid (n per cell 6, 12, 16, 24, 32; 1 or 2 placements; placement ICC 0.3 or 0.6) reaches 0.8. The best is 0.06 (Opus 4.8, 32 × 2, ICC 0.3); Sonnet 5 never exceeds 0.01.

  - **C-frag:** per-document `O_diff`.
  - **C-split:** the isolated multi-document dossier. ## G. Deviations and notes

1. **Not sized at central priors, and the grid was not capped at the inventory.** The inventory supports fewer than 12 per cell, so a grid capped at it would be empty. The grid runs n in {6, 12, 16, 24, 32} (6 is the cap with reuse), read as conditional on a repair.
2. **Scores are not simulated in the main grid.** Flags are drawn at each view's recall. The 5-point grid with the randomised tie is checked on one design (D.4) and in a unit test.
3. **The (1,1) headroom comparator** is the control's per-document O_diff unit (a one-document reading). The brief's single control edits two documents; read by any of its two units, the bar rises (Opus 4.8 0.12, Sonnet 5 0.23). B4's brief should say which comparator it uses, or add a one-document control.
4. **Contrasts are raw differences**, as brief 19 B2 defines them, not sham-netted as the theory's Δ (section 3). Netting C-frag needs a one-document and a two-document control; flagged for B4's brief.
5. **The (1,1) cell's shape is open.** Constraint 3 (the item's two copies equal at the session's end, because the unequal-values rule is free) means a (1,1) test change cannot change the shared value in one copy only. B4's brief must fix its shape: both parts in one document with the shared value untouched, or the value changed in both copies with the second part in one document (which then puts the (1,1) test change under statistic A's rule too).
6. **Item effects are drawn independently per cell, and C-ws's two views are drawn independently given the test change.** Both choices are conservative. No estimate of the item ICC exists; T1's per-document spread under one judge (0 of 10 to 10 of 11 by document) suggests it is not small.
7. **Priors and thresholds.** Every prior was read at a lower threshold than B4-R's (section B). The planted units' scores that would let the recall be re-read at B4-R's thresholds sit only in judge caches, which this brief does not open.
8. **Window counts** (section A) use B4-R's construction (every day boundary to every later write); B4's own windows are for its brief.
9. **Found in development and fixed with a test:** the corpus is in file order with sessions interleaved, so a boundary's start and end times can run backwards. The account count now reads each boundary's span in both directions; no count changed on the corpus.
10. **The suite.** The new tests pass (18), and the fork-copy test passes with the two new files added to its list (65).
